      How it works

      1. Drop your PunkThe original PNG, a download from a marketplace or a phone screenshot. We find the 24×24 pixels and read the colours.
      2. Watch it buildPick Mini or XL. Each pixel becomes bricks and plates in real colours, and the bust builds itself in 3D, cap first set aside, then put on.
      3. Build it for realDownload the step-by-step instructions (PDF), the parts list and a BrickLink list you can upload to buy every piece.

      Questions

      Is my image uploaded anywhere?

      No. Everything happens in your browser: reading the image, designing the model, the 3D view, the PDF and the video. There is no server and no AI behind this site, and nothing about your Punk is stored or sent.

      Can I really build it?

      Every model is checked by computer: all pieces are standard parts in real colours, every piece is attached to the base (0 floating), no two pieces overlap (0 collisions) and the centre of mass sits over the base. The site shows these checks for your model and says so plainly if one fails. Models have not been built with real bricks yet, so treat your first build as a test. A few colours can be hard to find in large plates.

      How do I get the bricks?

      Download the BrickLink list (XML). On BrickLink, open Want → Upload, paste or upload the file, then buy from one or several sellers. The CSV parts list works in any spreadsheet.

      What are the clear pieces?

      Some Punks have details that touch nothing, like cigarette smoke. They are held up by small clear bricks, and the site tells you when it adds them.

      Is this official?

      No. Punk to Bricks is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the LEGO Group or the CryptoPunks project.
